Anxiety & Stress Counselling

Anxiety can manifest in many ways — from constant worrying and racing thoughts to physical symptoms like a tight chest, difficulty sleeping, or panic attacks. You might find yourself avoiding situations, over-planning, or struggling to switch off.

Using CBT and mindfulness-based techniques, I'll help you understand your anxiety patterns, challenge unhelpful thinking, and develop practical strategies you can use in everyday life. Many clients notice a significant reduction in their symptoms within 8-12 sessions.

  • Generalised anxiety disorder (GAD)
  • Panic attacks and panic disorder
  • Social anxiety
  • Health anxiety
  • Work-related stress and burnout
  • Phobias

Depression & Low Mood

Depression is more than feeling sad. It can drain your energy, steal your motivation, and make even simple tasks feel overwhelming. You might feel disconnected from the people and activities you used to enjoy, or experience persistent feelings of worthlessness or guilt.

Together, we'll explore the thoughts and behaviours that keep depression going and work to break those cycles. I use a combination of CBT, behavioural activation, and compassion-focused techniques to help you reconnect with what matters to you.

  • Persistent low mood
  • Loss of interest or pleasure
  • Fatigue and low motivation
  • Negative self-talk and self-criticism
  • Grief and bereavement
  • Postnatal depression

Relationship & Couples Therapy

Relationships are central to our wellbeing, but they can also be a source of deep pain. Whether you're experiencing communication breakdowns, trust issues, recurring arguments, or simply feeling disconnected from your partner, therapy can help.

I work with both individuals exploring relationship patterns and couples attending sessions together. Using integrative and emotionally-focused approaches, I help you understand each other's perspectives, express needs more effectively, and rebuild emotional connection.

  • Communication difficulties
  • Trust and infidelity
  • Conflict resolution
  • Emotional disconnection
  • Pre-marital counselling
  • Separation and divorce support

Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT)

CBT is one of the most extensively researched and effective forms of talking therapy. It's based on the principle that our thoughts, feelings, and behaviours are interconnected — and that by changing unhelpful thinking patterns, we can change how we feel and act.

In our CBT sessions, you'll learn to identify negative automatic thoughts, test them against reality, and develop healthier alternatives. I also incorporate practical homework exercises between sessions to help you build lasting skills.

  • Structured, goal-oriented approach
  • Typically 8-20 sessions
  • Evidence-based with strong research backing
  • Practical tools and techniques you keep for life
  • Suitable for anxiety, depression, OCD, and more

Online Therapy Sessions

I offer secure, confidential online therapy sessions via video call for clients who prefer the flexibility of remote sessions or who live outside Bristol. Online therapy is just as effective as face-to-face sessions for most people and most conditions.

All you need is a private space, a stable internet connection, and a device with a camera. I use an encrypted, GDPR-compliant video platform to ensure your privacy and confidentiality are fully protected.

  • Same quality of care as in-person sessions
  • Flexible scheduling including evenings
  • No travel time or parking worries
  • Ideal for busy professionals or parents
  • Available across the UK

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

If your question isn't answered here, please don't hesitate to get in touch.

How many sessions will I need?

This varies from person to person. Some clients find that 6-8 sessions of focused CBT are enough to address their concerns, while others benefit from longer-term work over several months. We'll discuss expectations during your initial consultation and regularly review your progress together.

What's the difference between counselling and psychotherapy?

In practice, the terms are often used interchangeably. Counselling tends to focus on specific, current issues and is typically shorter-term. Psychotherapy can explore deeper, long-standing patterns and may be longer-term. I offer both approaches and will recommend whichever is most appropriate for your situation.

Is everything I say confidential?

Yes, with very few exceptions. Confidentiality is a cornerstone of therapy. The only circumstances where I might need to share information are if there is a serious risk of harm to you or someone else, or if required by law. I'll explain this fully before we begin working together.

Do you offer evening or weekend appointments?

Yes. I understand that fitting therapy around work and family commitments can be challenging. I offer select evening appointments on Tuesdays and Thursdays, and Saturday morning sessions. These slots are popular, so I recommend booking early.

What is your cancellation policy?

I ask for at least 48 hours' notice for cancellations. Sessions cancelled with less than 48 hours' notice or missed without contact will be charged at the full session rate. I understand that genuine emergencies arise, and I'll always try to be flexible where possible.

Can I use my health insurance?

Some private health insurance providers do cover therapy sessions. I'm registered with several major insurers. Please check with your provider and mention my name and BACP registration number when enquiring about coverage.
