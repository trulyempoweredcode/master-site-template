About Me

Hello, I'm Sarah

I'm a BACP-registered counsellor and psychotherapist based in Clifton, Bristol. For over a decade, I've had the privilege of supporting people through some of the most challenging periods of their lives — and watching them emerge stronger, more self-aware, and more resilient.

I trained at the University of Bristol, completing a Doctorate in Counselling Psychology, and I've since built a practice grounded in warmth, respect, and evidence-based care. I specialise in Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT) but draw on integrative and person-centred approaches when they better suit a client's needs.

Outside of the therapy room, I'm a keen walker (the Bristol harbourside is my favourite route), a lifelong reader, and a slightly obsessive tea drinker. I believe that therapists are human too, and that connection starts with being genuine.

My Approach

How I Work With You

Therapy isn't one-size-fits-all. Here's what you can expect when we work together.

1

Initial Conversation

We start with a free 15-minute phone call so you can ask questions, share what's brought you to therapy, and see whether we feel like a good fit. There's no pressure to commit.

2

Assessment Session

In our first full session, I'll take time to understand your history, what you're experiencing now, and what you'd like to achieve. Together, we'll agree on a plan that feels right for you.

3

Ongoing Therapy

Sessions are typically weekly and last 50 minutes. I'll use CBT techniques alongside other evidence-based methods, always adapting to what works best for you as we progress.

4

Review & Ending

We'll regularly review your progress together. When you feel ready, we'll plan a gradual ending to therapy so you leave with confidence and the tools to maintain your wellbeing long-term.

My Values

What I Believe In

These principles guide every interaction I have with my clients.

Compassion First

You're not broken and you don't need fixing. I meet every client with genuine warmth and respect, exactly as they are. My role is to walk alongside you, not to judge.

Safety & Trust

Healing happens when you feel safe. I work hard to create a space where you can be open and vulnerable without fear. Everything shared in our sessions is confidential.

Inclusivity

My practice is open and affirming to people of all backgrounds, identities, and orientations. I believe everyone deserves access to compassionate mental health support.
