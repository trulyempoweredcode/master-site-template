How I Approach My Work

I work collaboratively, with shared responsibility for the process. I support each person's pace, without pressure to move faster than feels right.

I offer a consistent, professional space with clear boundaries. I prioritise emotional safety, especially when working with difficult material.

I lean in to openness and complexity rather than rushing to fix things. I work relationally rather than directive, unless we agree otherwise.

I hold the wider context of a person's life and relationships in mind. I'm clear about what I can and can't offer within this work.
