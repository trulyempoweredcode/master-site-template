Welcome

Therapy is an act of courage, not a sign of weakness

Taking the first step towards therapy can feel daunting, and I want you to know that reaching out takes real strength. Whether you're struggling with anxious thoughts that won't quiet down, a low mood that's hard to shift, or relationships that feel strained — you don't have to face it alone.

My practice offers a warm, non-judgemental space where we can work together at your pace. Using evidence-based approaches, I'll help you understand your patterns, develop practical coping strategies, and move towards the life you want to live.
